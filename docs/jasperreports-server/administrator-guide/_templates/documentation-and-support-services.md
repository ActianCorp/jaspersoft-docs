## How to Contact Support for Jaspersoft Products

You can contact the Support team in the following ways:

-   To access the Support Knowledge Base and getting personalized content about products you are interested in, visit our [product Support website](https://www.jaspersoft.com/support).
-   To create a Support case, you must have a valid maintenance or support contract with a Actian entity. You also need a username and password to log in to the [product Support website](https://www.jaspersoft.com/support). If you do not have a username, you can request one by clicking **Register** on the website.

## How to Join Jaspersoft Community

Jaspersoft Community is the official channel for Jaspersoft customers, partners, and employee subject matter experts to share and access their collective experience. Jaspersoft Community offers access to Q&A forums, product wikis, and best practices. It also offers access to extensions, adapters, solution accelerators, and tools that extend and enable customers to gain full value from Jaspersoft products. In addition, users can submit and vote on feature requests from within the [Jaspersoft Ideas Portal](https://jaspersoftideas.tibco.com/). For a free registration, go to [Jaspersoft Community](https://community.jaspersoft.com/).
