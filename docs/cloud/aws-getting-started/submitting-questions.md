# Submitting Questions

You have multiple resources to find answers to your questions:

-   Self-help: Research answers to your questions (Knowledge Search on the Customer Portal, Documentation, Community Forums, Community wiki, Online Learning Portal, etc.).

-   Post a question:

    -   Community Forums with the tag #AWS. This tag is tracked and followed up by Jaspersoft staff. This is free for those who have registered for Free Online Help for Jaspersoft for AWS. <http://community.jaspersoft.com/answers>
    -   ^C/S^ Submit a Case. This is for customers who have purchased annual subscriptions of incident-based or unlimited incident support.

Learn more <http://www.jaspersoft.com/support-options-table> .
