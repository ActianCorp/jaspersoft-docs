# 1.1 About This Document

This brief document will help you better understand the information and materials available to you, and tell you where to begin your search for information as you explore your Jaspersoft product(s) or project(s). Depending on your subscription, some or all of the sections of this document will be relevant to you. To facilitate your navigation of this document, we have marked those sections, pertinent only to annual subscription customers and those with incident-based support, with the superscript ^C/S^.

This document contains the following sections:

-   [Available Resources](available-resources.md) - A list of the various self-service and communication resources available
-   [Options for Working with Your Data](options-for-working-with-your.md)- Creating simple reports, dashboards, and visualizations in a web browser with JasperReports Server or more sophisticated reports with the downloadable Jaspersoft Studio Professional .
-   [Launching JasperReports Server (JRS) on AWS Marketplace](launching-jasperreports-server.md) - Launching JRS on AWS Marketplace and applying your BYOL (“Bring Your Own License”) key if required.
-   [Connecting to Your Data with JRS](connecting-to-your-data-with.md) - Jaspersoft can work with nearly any data source and makes connecting to Amazon data sources even easier.
-   [Jaspersoft Studio Professional (JSS Pro)](jaspersoft-studio-professional.md) - JSS Pro lets you create sophisticated, pixel-perfect reports on the desktop and then upload your reports to JasperReports Server on AWS for secured management, scheduling, and sharing.
-   [Product Documentation](product-documentation.md) – This section explains the purposes of the various guides supplied for our products, and provides suggestions to help you quickly become productive.
-   [Submitting Questions](submitting-questions.md) – This section contains an overview of how you can research answers to your questions, post questions to Community Forums, or Submit a Case to Customer Support.
-   ^C/S^ [Submitting a Case (annual subscription customers only)](c-s-submitting-a-case-annual.md)– This section contains recommendations and best practices for when submitting a case.
